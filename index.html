1. How would you like to calculate labor? For example, if printing takes 10 minutes, cutting takes 15 minutes, and packaging takes 5 minutes: - A. Set an hourly rate for each task separately. - B. Use one general hourly rate for all tasks. - C. Support both options. My recommendation: C, so you can choose per product. 2. What supplier information should we store? My recommendation is supplier name, contact number, Facebook page or website, address, product reference/link, and notes. Would you also like to record purchase history and supplier-specific prices? 3. How should selling prices work? - A. Enter your selling price manually. - B. Automatically suggest a price based on your desired profit margin. - C. Both: suggest a price, but allow you to override it. My recommendation: C. 4. How should tax be handled? - A. Show prices before tax only. - B. Add an optional tax percentage. - C. Configure tax settings in Business Settings and use them in the calculator. My recommendation: C, with tax optional so you can leave it at zero when it doesn't apply.
