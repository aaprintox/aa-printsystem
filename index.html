THE NUMBER CRUNCHER

Product Costing & Pricing

Build a price with confidence. Follows the 8-step calculation order from direct costs to final minimum charge.

Examples only—not fixed shop rates. Adjust every amount to match your actual costs.

How this works

List each material you buy: the pack price, how many sheets come in a pack, and how many finished pieces one sheet makes. The calculator works out the cost per piece and for the whole job. Unrounded costs stay accurate internally and round up at the end of the calculation order.

Markup adds % above cost; Profit Margin sets profit as a % of selling price.

Materials & costs for this job

Step 1 · Direct costs

Add one line per material. Pick a saved price from your library or type it in. Use “direct cost” for flat amounts such as ink or packaging.

Materials library

Save each material’s pack price once, then reuse it in any calculation. Change a price here and every linked line updates.

Advanced options

Waste, quantity discount, minimum charge, labor, and overhead. Anything you set here is included in the price, even while this section is closed.

Enter a custom price to override the suggested price. Cost and profit will recalculate.

Applied after markup/margin, protected by a minimum 5% margin floor.

Step 2: Spoilage/trim allowance on direct materials & packaging.

Leave blank to use Business Settings minimum charge; enter 0 to waive.

Step 3 · Labor

Toggle on to factor your time into this job. Solo owners who keep this off are never blocked.

Formula: Monthly income you want ÷ hours you work per month (e.g., ₱20,000 ÷ 160 hrs = ₱125/hr).

Total owner hours spent prepping, printing, cutting, and packing.

Choose one general rate for all work, or enter separate rates and time for printing, cutting, packaging, and layout.

Optional batch cost for hired assistants/staff, kept separate from owner rate.

＋ Step 4: Overhead & fees (optional)

Applied after labor in the calculation order. Leave at zero if not needed for this job.

After saving it as a product, you can select it in the Quotations tab.
